# 🛡️ CyberGuard AI: Intelligent Online Harassment Awareness & Protection Platform

An AI-powered cybersecurity, threat analysis, and digital safety platform designed to assist victims of online harassment, provide step-by-step legal guidance, and offer mental health support.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **Smart AI Content Detector:** Analyzes suspicious text, emails, or messages for toxicity and matches appropriate safety guidelines.
- **Comprehensive Harassment Guide Hub:** Covers Cyberbullying, Doxxing, Cyberstalking, Deepfakes, Sextortion, Hate Speech, and more with instant mitigation steps.
- **24/7 AI Safety Assistant:** Interactive chat to guide users through digital privacy and emergency protocols.
- **Official Government Portals & Video Hub:** Direct links to `cybercrime.gov.in`, helpline `1930`, and verified awareness tutorials.
- **Multilingual Support:** English, Hindi, and Marathi accessibility.
